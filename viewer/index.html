grazer (brighter = more energy, fainter = more camouflage or deeper in cover) canopy (forest: the only ground that hides a grazer from hunters - and almost nothing grows under it) perception (how far this animal can actually see - free out to 6 tiles, then one spoke on the ring for every energy/tick it burns keeping the extra horizon) flight distance (the ground a grazer keeps between itself and one particular hunter: a few ticks of warning times that hunter's speed, so it is tight around a slow predator and enormous around a fast one - a ghost-faint ring means nothing is in sight and this is what it would keep) hungry nerve (amber: this grazer is starving and letting hunters come closer than it otherwise would) herd bond (linked grazers are harder for a hunter to single out - brighter = deeper cover) gregariousness (how strongly a grazer walks to company instead of food) provisioning (the rose core, on either species: how much of itself this animal pours into each newborn instead of its own survival - no energy is created, it only moves down a generation) hunter (brighter = more energy) acuity (rings around a hunter - sees through camouflage, herds and canopy, costs energy every tick) menace (the wide sparse ring: how much ground this hunter's speed alone clears of feeding grazers, whether or not it ever catches one) resource (grows where light reaches the ground, so it clusters in the open) carcass (a grazer's body - the only carrion a hunter can eat; fades as it rots) hunter remains (crossed out: predators no longer live off their own dead, so this is scenery) migrant (just arrived, carries remembered genes)
